Our Mission

For decades, traditional banks have ignored the informal sector, imposing high hardware costs and monthly rental fees that excluded spaza shops, street vendors, and independent mechanics. Our mission is to democratise digital commerce by utilising the technology everyone already has: the smartphone.

The Innovation

By pivoting away from expensive NFC/Card tapping and embracing Instant EFT (Electronic Funds Transfer) and Dynamic QR codes, we bypass the legacy 3.5% interchange fees. We pass these massive savings directly to the merchant, empowering them to grow their businesses safely, without the risk of holding cash.

Why Instant EFT is Winning

Traditional POS hardware is too expensive for a fruit vendor or small spaza. Card interchange fees (averaging 3.5%) eat directly into their profits. However, with 79% of South Africans now using smartphones, banking apps are universally accessible.

Blink Pay leverages this. By replacing hardware with a simple QR code and routing payments through Instant EFT rails, we collapse the cost structure, making digital payments cheaper and safer than handling cash.

2. Personal Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum personal information necessary to provide our services (principle of minimality under Section 10 of POPIA):

Merchant Onboarding: Full name, business/spaza name, phone number, city or township.
Transaction Data: Payment amounts, timestamps, and merchant/payer identifiers required for settlement and reconciliation.
Technical Data: Device type, IP address, and app usage analytics to maintain platform security and performance.
We do NOT collect: Customer banking credentials, card numbers, PINs, or passwords. These remain entirely within your banking app.

3. Purpose of Collection

We collect personal information for specific, defined, and lawful purposes (Section 13 of POPIA — Purpose Specification):

To onboard and verify merchants on the Blink Pay network.
To process, settle, and reconcile Instant EFT payments.
To communicate service updates, onboarding status, and support via WhatsApp or email.
To comply with FICA, SARB, and other applicable South African financial regulations.
To detect and prevent fraud, money laundering, and unauthorised access.
To improve our platform and services through anonymised analytics.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We share personal information only where strictly necessary to deliver our service or comply with the law. We never sell personal data to third parties.

BankservAfrica: Transaction routing data shared via their Immediate Payment (ImPay) rails for settlement processing. BankservAfrica is itself a regulated entity under SARB.
Participating Banks: Minimum data necessary to complete inter-bank EFT instructions (e.g., account number, branch code, amount).
Law Enforcement / Regulators: When required by law, court order, or SARB/FSCA/FIC directive.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes stated above, or as required by law (Section 14 of POPIA — Retention Limitation).

Transaction Records: Minimum 5 years as required by FICA (Financial Intelligence Centre Act).
Merchant Onboarding Data: Duration of active account plus 5 years after account closure.
Marketing Contact Information: Until you withdraw consent or unsubscribe, whichever is earlier.
Technical / Log Data: 90 days for security monitoring purposes.

6. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under POPIA (Part 3 of Chapter 2), you have the following rights regarding your personal information held by Blink Pay:

Right of Access

Request a copy of all personal information we hold about you.

Right to Correction

Request that inaccurate or incomplete information be corrected or updated.

Right to Deletion

Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention obligations.

Right to Object

Object to the processing of your personal information for direct marketing or other purposes.

Right to Withdraw Consent

Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on your consent. Withdrawal does not affect prior processing.

Right to Complain

Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa if you believe your rights have been violated.

To exercise any of the above rights, contact our Information Officer at privacy@blinkpay.co.za. We will respond within 30 days of receiving your request.
